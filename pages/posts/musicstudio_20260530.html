Final Thoughts

Flaws

As mentioned before, I was working with a room full of miscellaneous gear, alot of which was rescued from a flood. That being said, I went into this with tempered expectations; I had some ideals of how I wanted to set things up however not everything went to plan or works correctly.

  • Kinda minor but I was going to set up the racks as such; all effects in one rack, all the odd-ball stuff like the interface and patch bays in the other. Grouped logically I figured. In fact, you can see in the photos above of the setting up stage I had all the effects in one. However, the pic of the patch bays and the whole set up, there's some effects in the other. This was because the three signex isopatch bays are hard wired; the cables are soldered on and some of them were simply too short to reach between the two racks. Hence why the three gate units are just in with the "util" rack and not with the other effects. This obv isn't a real issue but I am planning on teaching people some music production/general audio and podcasting and it would have been a bit nicer for newbies if that stuff was a bit more logically set up.
  • The mixer has a few things wrong with it. The analog VU meters at the top right both don't work. One is smashed and ye the other just doesn't move despite being intact. There's also a faulty fader on channel twenty two which acts like a toggle lmao that is, at -∞db its silent and then anything above that is full volume lol might as well be an extra mute button. But that's the only channel with issues.
  • The patch bay I'm using for the effect send/returns had a broken connector or something lodged in output eighteen. I'll need to surgically remove that at some point when I have pliers. And input one of the bay I'm using for the outs of the interface and ins to the mixer is just broken.
  • My plan was to do hybrid analog; record into the computer then output all the individual daw tracks to the mixer channels for mixing and mastering using the outboard gear. As shown above, the audio interface we have has eight in/outs (with extra two for monitors). We also have the expander octopre thing which is basically the same: eight in and out. I (wrongfully) assumed that meant I would have sixteen outs to run from the daw through the mixer simultaneously. Sixteen tracks for a whole song is usually plenty/not too shabby. Nooope. The octopre is just an input expander. The outputs on it are just zero latency copies of the inputs for monitoring. i.e. you can't send anything to them over the adat connection from the main interface... so I only have eight outs from the daw lmao. Which is a bit disappointing.
